Reading the evidence

Bisection and safeguarded Newton require opposite endpoint signs unless an endpoint evaluates to zero. A polynomial is continuous, but floating-point cancellation can make computed signs unreliable. This tool does not use interval arithmetic and does not certify every root.

A small residual does not bound the distance to a root, especially near a flat or repeated root. Bracket termination returns the midpoint. Newton can diverge or cycle; secant can encounter equal function values. Safeguarded Newton accepts a Newton proposal only in the inner 80% of the current bracket, otherwise bisecting. It is not Brent's method.

Coefficients are constant first, degree 1–8, bounded by one million. Input x values are within ±1000; numerical excursions beyond ±10¹² stop the affected method. The update budget is at most 200. Data stays in your browser unless exported.
